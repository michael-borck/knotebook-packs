# Store policy

Short version: you must have the right to share the content, and the pack must be
safe to store as data.

## Requirements

- You own the story and assets, or have permission to distribute them.
- The pack declares a licence.
- No executable content, scripts, or arbitrary HTML/JS.
- No personal data, harassment, or illegal content.
- Metadata (title, author, description, theme) is accurate.

## Review and signing

The maintainer reviews each pack, then signs it with the store key and rebuilds
the catalogue. A merged pull request is not live until the store deploy completes.

## Versions and corrections

Corrections ship as a new version of the same pack id. The previous version is
replaced in the catalogue.

## Takedown

If a pack is reported, it is removed from the catalogue and the store is rebuilt.
Repeated or serious violations can lead to a contributor being blocked.

## Disclaimer

The store is curated but not a legal review. Authors are responsible for their
content and its licence.
